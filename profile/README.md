# Balford — Zhejiang Balford Mechatronics Co., Ltd.

Deep drawn metal housings, solenoid valve housings and precision stamped components for
OEM buyers. We design and build our own tooling, run presses from 25 t to 350 t, and deep
draw to a maximum diameter of Ø250 mm in a 12,000 m² plant in Zhuji, Zhejiang, China.

Zhejiang Balford Mechatronics Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 2017, continuing a toolmaking
business that has been building single-hit drawing dies since 2000 (Ruian Derlin Stampings
Factory) — more than 4,000 dies kept and reused.

**Website:** https://balford.net

## What we publish here

Command-line calculators and design checks our DFM engineers use on real enquiries —
die clearance, draw ratios, stamping force, bend allowance, blank weight, springback.
The toolset sits in the [`balford`](https://github.com/balford-mechatronics/balford)
repository.
